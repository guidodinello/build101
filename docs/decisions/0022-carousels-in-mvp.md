# 0022: Carousels with text slides are in the MVP

- Date: 2026-10-17
- Status: proposed

## Context

The MVP listed carousels as *Won't* (F4.7 in [mvp.md](../product/mvp.md); now F4.9) and the demo was one image Post ([ADR 0016](0016-demo-scope-one-post.md)). The team now wants the Owner to be able to ask the agent for an Instagram carousel in the chat and get it generated by the image service chosen in [ADR 0018](0018-ai-image-generation-service.md) and [ADR 0019](0019-image-provider-gemini.md).

A carousel is only worth making if its slides read as one piece, and the useful carousels for a small business (cover, a few points, a call to action) carry text. ADR 0018 forbade any text on generated images, because the model can invent prices or offers.

Alternatives considered:

- **Photo-only slides, text in the caption.** Keeps the no-text rule, but a carousel without headlines is just a photo album.
- **Several full carousels to pick from**, like image Variations. Two to three times the cost and wait for every request.
- **One multi-turn chat with the model, one slide per turn.** Google's recommended way to iterate, but the slides run one after another and every turn resends the growing history.
- **Gemini's Interactions API** (`client.interactions.create`), the call shape in the current docs. It is Beta with breaking changes and stores interactions for 55 days by default; Google recommends `generateContent` for production.

## Decision

- **Carousels are *Must*.** The Owner asks for one in the chat; the agents choose 3 to 5 slides, or the number the Owner asks for, up to 10 (Instagram's limit). Image Posts with Variations stay as they are.
- **Slides are designed slides with text.** The text on every slide is written by the Copywriter and stored in the Post's copy; the Art Director only writes the look and the scene of each slide. Code puts the exact text into each image prompt and forbids any other text, prices, numbers or logos. The Owner's real product must stay unchanged.
- **One carousel per request.** The Owner approves it, discards it, or asks to redo specific slides ("cambiá la 3"); there are no Variations for carousels.
- **Consistency by anchoring:** the cover is generated first; the other slides are generated in parallel, each with the cover as a style reference next to the product photos. Redoing a slide anchors on the current cover (redoing the cover anchors on slide 2).
- **The rendered text is checked** by a small Claude vision call against the expected text; a slide that doesn't match is regenerated once. This is the only LLM check of a generated image.
- **Gemini is called through `generateContent`** (`client.aio.models.generate_content`) with `ImageConfig(aspect_ratio="4:5", image_size="2K")`, not through the Interactions API.

## Consequences

- Cost and wait grow with the slide count: about $0.05 per 2K slide plus input tokens, so roughly $0.30 to $0.40 and two generation rounds (an estimated 40 to 70 s) for 5 slides. Both are measured on the first real calls.
- The "no text on the image" rule from ADR 0018 now applies to image Posts only. On carousels, text is allowed only when it comes from the copy, and the model can still misspell it; the text check and the Owner's Approval ([ADR 0007](0007-approve-every-post.md)) are the safeguards.
- New schema: `posts.format` gains `carousel`, a `post_slides` table holds the slides per revision, and the publisher takes several images.
- New Copywriter and Art Director carousel modes, a Text Checker agent, a `redo_slides` job and orchestrator tool, and new WhatsApp copy for sending the slides in order.
- The WhatsApp flow is longer: the slides arrive as numbered images before the preview.
