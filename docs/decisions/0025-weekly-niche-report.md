# 0025: Weekly Niche Report from grounded web search

- Date: 2026-10-18
- Status: proposed

## Context

Posts are written from the Brand Brain and the Owner's brief only. Nothing tells the Agency what is working in the Owner's niche right now, and the Art Director's **trend notes** input has had no source ([open questions](../open-questions.md#post-images-generated-by-ai-adr-0018)). The team wants a weekly analysis of the niche (what goes viral, which formats and angles work, what to talk about) that feeds post and carousel generation, starting with curated solid-wood furniture and home stores in Uruguay (like La Ibérica), the MVP's vertical ([mvp.md](../product/mvp.md)).

Sources compared in [research/niche-trend-sources.md](../research/niche-trend-sources.md):

- **Gemini with Grounding with Google Search**: no new vendor ([ADR 0023](0023-gemini-text-llm.md)), 5,000 free search requests per month for Gemini 3+ models, then US$14 per 1,000. No engagement numbers; the web is English- and US-centric.
- **Instagram Business Discovery**: real likes, comments and views of named Business or Creator accounts, but no saves or shares, and only under Instagram API with Facebook Login, which reopens [ADR 0015](0015-instagram-login.md) and needs App Review to serve other Businesses.
- **Scrapers**: break Instagram's terms on automated collection. Hashtag Search, the Meta Ad Library and the Google Trends API don't fit (review and quotas, no commercial ads in Uruguay, closed alpha).

## Decision

- A **Niche Report** per Business: a short structured summary of what is working in its niche this week (themes, formats, hooks, commercial dates ahead), each point with its source links.
- Generated by a new Specialist, the **Niche Analyst**, as a single Gemini call with **Grounding with Google Search**, through `backend/app/llm/client.py`. Input: the Business's `category`, location and Brand Brain (products, audience), plus a fixed list of search themes per niche, starting with curated solid-wood furniture and home stores in Uruguay (the La Ibérica segment) ([research/furniture-decor-uruguay.md](../research/furniture-decor-uruguay.md)).
- Runs **weekly** in the in-process job runner ([ADR 0005](0005-single-instance-in-process-jobs.md)) and on demand when the Owner asks ("¿qué está funcionando en mi rubro?"). The weekly run is silent: the Owner sees the report only when they ask, since messages outside the 24-hour window are Won't in the MVP (F1.8).
- The latest report is the **trend notes** input of the Content Strategist, the Copywriter and the Art Director.
- Instagram Business Discovery is left for later; scrapers are not used.
- Priority **Should** in [mvp.md](../product/mvp.md) (F3.6, F3.7): the demo of [ADR 0016](0016-demo-scope-one-post.md) does not depend on it.

## Consequences

- Trend notes may change the angle, format and styling of a Post, never its facts: prices, discounts, dates and claims still come only from the Brand Brain, the Catalog, Offers and the Owner's words. Commercial dates come from the static list the Content Strategist already uses, not from the search.
- Reference posts found by the search are described, never copied: Instagram cuts the reach of unoriginal content ([research/instagram-content-trends.md](../research/instagram-content-trends.md#how-instagram-ranks-content)).
- Cost: one grounded call per Business per week, plus on-demand calls; each call can run several billed searches. Grounding for Gemini 3+ is not on the free tier, so the key must have billing linked (already required by [ADR 0023](0023-gemini-text-llm.md)).
- The report reflects what the web writes about the niche, not measured engagement. Business Discovery can add numbers later behind the same report shape, after ADR 0015 is revisited.
- How good grounded search is for a Uruguayan niche in Spanish is not verified yet; a hands-on run comes before building.
