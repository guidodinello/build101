# Platform, operations and testing

This doc covers the platform, operations and testing dimension of the 2026-10-19 product and codebase audit of Neitor AI (see [README.md](README.md) for the full audit and [summary.md](summary.md) for the cross-dimension synthesis): hosting, deploys, durability, monitoring, backups, environments, CI and the test suite. The code was read at commit `dc0bc01`. Every finding below was checked by an independent adversarial reviewer against the code and the cited sources; "confirmed" means the reviewer agreed, and "adjusted" means the reviewer corrected the severity or details. The severities and recommendations here are the corrected ones. A production readiness checklist derived from the findings sits before the findings themselves.

## Summary

The engineering basics are sound for a hackathon MVP. The uv lock is frozen in Docker ([Dockerfile:10,17](../../../backend/Dockerfile)), CI has a path-filtered aggregate gate that the main ruleset requires ([ci.yml:144-151](../../../.github/workflows/ci.yml); ruleset observed in the audit, 2026-10-19), there are 591 fast backend unit tests plus 95 Postgres tests in CI (audit observation, 2026-10-19), migrations run as a Railway pre-deploy step ([backend/README.md:61](../../../backend/README.md)), and webhooks are HMAC-verified ([kapso_webhook.py:12-17](../../../backend/app/channels/kapso_webhook.py)).

Operations are the gap:

- **The product cannot yet survive its own deploys.** Post drafts, Post images, the webhook dedupe set and the daily cost cap live only in memory (OPS-1, OPS-6). Railway redeploys on every push to main (71 commits in 3 days) and kills in-flight generations, publishes and jobs within seconds, with no drain and no notice to the Owner (OPS-2).
- **It cannot tell the team when it fails.** There is no error tracking, uptime monitor or alerting (OPS-4), no staging environment (OPS-8), no database backup (OPS-7), and no record of LLM and image cost: the `agent_runs` table exists but nothing writes to it (OPS-9).
- **The production path is not ready** (OPS-3, OPS-12). The Kapso sandbox only messages registered phones, every Owner would publish to one demo Instagram account, Meta App Review has not started, Railway is still on its 30-day Trial, Vercel is on Hobby (non-commercial use only) and Supabase is on Free (no backups).

The first 10-50 real Owners would mostly feel this as silence: drafts that cannot be approved after a deploy, onboarding stuck on "generating brand", and failures nobody on the team sees. Most fixes are S or M effort and should land before the pilot.

### OPS-3: Production-readiness blockers from sandbox to real Owners (WhatsApp number, per-Owner Instagram, Meta review, plans)

- **Severity**: critical · **Effort**: L · **Confidence**: high · **Verified**: adjusted
- **Evidence**: `WHATSAPP_BOT_NUMBER` is the shared Kapso sandbox number, which "only answers phones with an active sandbox session" ([backend/README.md:78](../../../backend/README.md)); the sandbox serves only the registered phone, sends no templates and is "not intended" for production ([Kapso sandbox docs](https://docs.kapso.ai/docs/how-to/whatsapp/use-sandbox-for-testing.md), read 2026-10-19); one `ZERNIO_ACCOUNT_ID` for every Owner ([ADR 0024:15](../../decisions/0024-mvp-single-demo-instagram-account.md), [factory.py:48-50](../../../backend/app/conversation/factory.py)); Advanced Access, App Review and Business Verification not started ([open-questions.md:92](../../open-questions.md)), and the production number and templates are open ([open-questions.md:18](../../open-questions.md)); App Review and the production number are scheduled for Phase 2 ([roadmap.md:26](../../roadmap.md)); the Railway account is still on the 30-day Trial, created around 2026-10-17 ([ADR 0017:25,40](../../decisions/0017-backend-host-railway.md)); Hobby is non-commercial only ([Vercel fair use guidelines](https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines), read 2026-10-19) while the project is on Hobby ([frontend/README.md:32](../../../frontend/README.md)); Supabase Free has no backups, 500 MB of database and 1 GB of storage ([Supabase pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing), read 2026-10-19).
- **Problem**: The roadmap puts the production number and Meta review in Phase 2, but the next step is a pilot with real Owners, and each of these is a hard blocker. The sandbox cannot talk to an unregistered Owner. Every approved Post would go to the team's demo Instagram account. Our Meta app can only read Instagram testers. The Railway Trial ends around mid-November and the service stops. Charging Basic US$29 while hosting the landing and the `/api/render` route on Vercel Hobby breaks Vercel's terms. Meta Business Verification and App Review take weeks of calendar time, so starting late delays the pilot.
- **Recommendation**: Hard pilot blockers: (1) a production WhatsApp number on Kapso (Free includes 1 number), keeping the sandbox for development; (2) per-Business Zernio profiles and accounts in `social_accounts` (T27; `ensure_profile` and `connect_url` already exist; accounts beyond 2 cost $6 each), which needs an ADR superseding [ADR 0024](../../decisions/0024-mvp-single-demo-instagram-account.md); (3) move Railway to Hobby before the $5 trial credit or the 30 days run out. Start Meta Business Verification and App Review for `instagram_business_basic` now as long-lead items, and meanwhile add pilot Owners as Instagram Testers. Move Vercel to Pro before charging. Put a custom API domain in front of Railway. Track all of this as a go-live checklist (see [Production readiness checklist](#production-readiness-checklist)).
- **Impact**: Without these the pilot cannot start, or it publishes Owners' content to the wrong account. Starting Meta review now likely saves 2-6 weeks of calendar time (estimate).
- **Verifier note**: Most of this checks out. Kapso's docs, re-checked, mark "Multiple recipients" as not supported in Sandbox and say "The sandbox is for testing message flows, not production features", so a multi-Owner pilot is impossible on it. ADR 0024 and `factory.py` publish every Business to one env `ZERNIO_ACCOUNT_ID`, the Meta app reads only testers, and the roadmap puts App Review and the production number in Phase 2. ADR 0017 confirms the Trial is a one-time $5 or 30 days, whichever comes first, so it may end before mid-November. Vercel's fair-use page restricts Hobby to non-commercial use, and advertising paid plans counts as commercial. Overstated: not every item is a hard blocker. Per-Owner publishing through Zernio uses Zernio's own reviewed Meta app, so it needs no App Review on our side; a small pilot can read Instagram by adding Owners as Instagram Testers in development mode; an unverified WhatsApp business can still message, with limits; and Vercel Pro and Supabase Pro matter only once the team charges or holds real data (OPS-7, OPS-12). The hard blockers are the sandbox number, the single Zernio account and the Trial expiry, and critical stands for those.

### OPS-1: Post drafts, Post images and the daily cap are in memory, and every push to main wipes them

- **Severity**: high · **Effort**: L · **Confidence**: high · **Verified**: adjusted
- **Evidence**: `photos, store = InMemoryPhotos(), InMemoryPosts()` ([factory.py:47](../../../backend/app/conversation/factory.py)); `InMemoryPosts` lives "until T19 moves them onto posts" ([store.py:63-64](../../../backend/app/posts/store.py)); `PublicImages` is "In memory until T17 ... lost on restart" ([public.py:11-14](../../../backend/app/media/public.py)); `_generating` and `_designs_today` (the `MAX_POSTS_PER_DAY` counter) are process dicts ([maker.py:105-106](../../../backend/app/posts/maker.py)); Zernio fetches Post images from the in-memory `/media` URLs ([publish.py:134-136](../../../backend/app/posts/publish.py)); "State kept in memory is lost on every deploy or restart: ... served post images, in-memory photo and post stores" ([backend/README.md:101](../../../backend/README.md)); a push to main deploys ([backend/README.md:48](../../../backend/README.md), [setup.md:136](../../implementation/setup.md)); git log shows 71 commits on main from 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-19, only 31 touching `backend/` (audit observation, 2026-10-19), and the Railway settings table sets no watch paths ([backend/README.md:54-65](../../../backend/README.md)); the `posts`, `post_slides` and `post_variations` tables exist ([0001_initial.sql](../../../backend/migrations/versions/0001_initial.sql)) but have no ORM model ([models.py](../../../backend/app/db/models.py) has no `Post` class).
- **Problem**: Every redeploy, out-of-memory kill or crash drops all pending drafts and their JPEGs, and Railway redeploys the backend on every push to main, docs-only commits included. An Owner who gets a preview, then taps "Aprobar" or writes "subilo" after a deploy, finds the Post gone. A publish that is still resolving (Zernio answered "publishing"; [integrations.md:208](../../architecture/integrations.md)) loses its status. Zernio or WhatsApp fetching `/media/<token>.jpg` after a restart gets a 404, so Instagram rejects the Post. The per-day design cap, the only cost guard, resets on every deploy. Scheduled publishing (sold in Basic) is impossible while images live only in RAM.
- **Recommendation**: Frame this as pulling forward the already-planned T17, T19 and T30 work, not as a new design. Now (S): set Railway Watch Paths to `/backend/**` and deploy backend changes outside Uruguayan business hours during the pilot. Before the pilot: persist Post rows in the existing `posts` and `post_slides` tables, upload rendered JPEGs to the public `renders` bucket and send those URLs to Kapso and Zernio, and count designs per Business from `posts`. The last one is also needed to enforce the [ADR 0031](../../decisions/0031-pricing-free-basic-pro.md) Free cap and scheduled publishing.
- **Impact**: Removes the most likely pilot failure: a lost draft or failed publish on every deploy (about 10 a day during active development). Enables scheduled Posts and real enforcement of the Free tier's 3 Posts a month (ADR 0031).
- **Verifier note**: Verified in code: `factory.py` builds the in-memory stores, `store.py` and `public.py` say the data is lost on restart, `maker.py` keeps the counters as process dicts, `publish.py` builds the Zernio URLs from `public_base_url` plus `/media` paths, and `db/models.py` has no `Post`, `Message` or `AgentRun` model although the tables exist. Git shows 69 commits on main since 2026-10-17, 30 touching `backend/` (close to the 71/31 claimed), and the README settings table has no Watch Paths. Two parts are overstated. This is a known, documented gap with planned tasks (T17 renders bucket, T19 and T30 move to the DB), not a new discovery. The Zernio 404 case only happens if a restart lands mid-publish, because after a restart the Post is gone before any publish can start. Brand, history and Owner notes already survive deploys ([ADR 0030](../../decisions/0030-orchestrator-memory-and-context.md)). The real cost is lost drafts and approvals plus a reset daily cap, and an Owner can get a Post back by asking again (at generation cost). Serious for a pilot but not data-destroying, and Watch Paths plus off-hours deploys mitigate most of it cheaply. Downgraded to high.

### OPS-2: Deploys kill in-flight generations, publishes and jobs with no drain, and the Owner is never told

- **Severity**: high · **Effort**: S · **Confidence**: high · **Verified**: adjusted
- **Evidence**: The lifespan stops only the `JobRunner`, and `PostMaker.join`, `PostPublisher.join` and the `ChatHandler` tasks are never awaited at shutdown ([main.py:27-38](../../../backend/app/main.py)); fire-and-forget asyncio tasks ([maker.py:199](../../../backend/app/posts/maker.py), [publish.py:110](../../../backend/app/posts/publish.py), [chat.py:577](../../../backend/app/conversation/chat.py)); `stop()` cancels running jobs immediately ([runner.py:81-86](../../../backend/app/jobs/runner.py)); `fail_stale` marks them failed 5 minutes later but never calls `_tell_owner`, unlike runner.py:186-187 ([runner.py:88-109](../../../backend/app/jobs/runner.py)); `test_a_failed_first_brand_stays_generating` shows the Owner stays in `generating_brand` ([test_jobs.py:400](../../../backend/tests/test_jobs.py)); uvicorn runs with no `--timeout-graceful-shutdown` ([Dockerfile:35](../../../backend/Dockerfile)); Railway sends SIGTERM, then SIGKILL after `RAILWAY_DEPLOYMENT_DRAINING_SECONDS` ([Railway deployment teardown](https://docs.railway.com/deployments/deployment-teardown), read 2026-10-19), whose default is reported as 0-3 s ([Railway community answer](https://station.railway.com/questions/railway-deployment-draining-seconds-and-b76587af), read 2026-10-19).
- **Problem**: A Post takes 30-60 s and a brand plus Instagram import 30-100 s, but Railway gives the old container about 0-3 s after SIGTERM, so any deploy in that window cancels the work. A `generate_brand` job ends "failed: interrupted by a restart" without the `job_failed` message or retry button, so the Owner's onboarding is stuck in `generating_brand` with no way out. A Post generation simply never arrives. A publish task can die after Zernio accepted it, leaving the Owner without confirmation.
- **Recommendation**: (1) Set `RAILWAY_DEPLOYMENT_DRAINING_SECONDS` to about 120-150 and add `--timeout-graceful-shutdown` just below it. (2) On SIGTERM, stop the job poller first, then await the running jobs plus `PostMaker.join`, `PostPublisher.join` and the `ChatHandler` tasks with a deadline. (3) In `fail_stale`, requeue interrupted jobs (add an `attempts` column, max 2), or at least send `JOB_FAILED` with the retry button for kinds in `NOTIFY_ON_FAILURE`. (4) Add a test for a cancelled `generate_brand`.
- **Impact**: Removes a rare but permanent onboarding dead end and lost in-flight Posts, for under a day of work. The auditor's original framing, about 10 silent failures a day during active development turned into zero, assumes work is always in flight at deploy time, which the reviewer judged inflated.
- **Verifier note**: Verified: the lifespan stops only the `JobRunner`, the router lifespan only closes HTTP clients, and the `join` methods exist ("tests, shutdown") but nothing calls them at shutdown. `runner.stop()` cancels running jobs; `CancelledError` is a `BaseException`, so `_run` does not catch it, and the row stays `running` until `fail_stale` marks it failed about 5 minutes later without telling the Owner. `complete_onboarding` will not re-enqueue once a link is completed, and `BrandFlow.revise` refuses outside `REVISABLE_STATES`, so an interrupted Owner stays in `generating_brand` for good. Railway's official teardown page does not state a default draining time; forum and staff answers say 0 to 3 s, so the claim holds on a weak source. The impact is inflated for a few pilot Owners; the permanently stuck onboarding is the severe part. Downgraded to high, and the fix is still small. Missed by the auditor: while uvicorn drains, the old instance's poller keeps claiming new jobs until lifespan shutdown cancels them, so "stop claiming first" is needed.
- **Related**: [BUG-2](bugs-reliability.md), [ARCH-3](backend-architecture.md), [ARCH-2](backend-architecture.md), [BUG-4](bugs-reliability.md); OPS-6 in this doc

### OPS-4: No error tracking, uptime monitoring or alerting: the team won't know when an Owner's Post fails

- **Severity**: high · **Effort**: S · **Confidence**: high · **Verified**: adjusted
- **Evidence**: No `sentry-sdk` or OpenTelemetry dependency ([pyproject.toml:6-18](../../../backend/pyproject.toml)), and a grep finds neither in `backend/app` or `frontend/package.json`; handler failures after the 200 are only `logger.exception` ([router.py:72-79](../../../backend/app/channels/router.py)); publish crashes are only logged ([publish.py:117-121](../../../backend/app/posts/publish.py)); job failures notify the Owner but never the team ([runner.py:169,186-187](../../../backend/app/jobs/runner.py)); all lines land as `level=error` in Railway, so nothing can be alerted on ([backend/README.md:93](../../../backend/README.md)); log retention on Trial and Hobby is 7 days ([Railway logs guide](https://docs.railway.com/guides/logs), read 2026-10-19); Sentry's free Developer plan has 5k errors and 1 user ([Sentry pricing](https://sentry.io/pricing/), read 2026-10-19); Better Stack's free plan has 10 monitors and heartbeats, 1 status page, and Slack and email alerts ([Better Stack pricing](https://betterstack.com/pricing), read 2026-10-19).
- **Problem**: Every failure path (webhook handling, generation, render, publish, jobs, LLM 429 quota exhaustion) ends as a text line in Railway logs, where everything is tagged error and kept 7 days.
