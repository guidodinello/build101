# Unit economics and cost reduction

This doc covers what Neitor AI costs to run per Owner (text models, scene images, WhatsApp messages, Zernio publishing and the platform plans), the gross margin that leaves at the [ADR 0031](../../decisions/0031-pricing-free-basic-pro.md) prices, and the levers that bring the cost down. It is one dimension of the product and codebase audit of 2026-10-19 (index in [README.md](README.md), cross-dimension synthesis in [summary.md](summary.md)); the code was read at commit `dc0bc01`. An independent adversarial reviewer checked each finding against the code and the cited sources; "adjusted" means the reviewer corrected its severity or details, and the severity and recommendation below already include those corrections. In code link text, files under `backend/app/` drop that prefix, and other files (migrations, frontend) show only the file name; every link points at the full path. Amounts are US dollars (US$). **Every cost figure here is an estimate until per-call metering in `agent_runs` exists (COST-1).**

## Summary

- **Four lines drive most of the variable cost per Owner.** Images: scenes are generated at 2K, then downscaled to 1080 px before rendering, and every image "Cambiar" regenerates all slides (COST-5, COST-6). Zernio's per-account fee (COST-3). WhatsApp service messages, which Meta has billed since 2026-10-01; Neitor sends about 11 per 5-slide carousel (COST-7). Orchestrator calls: each resends about 35K tokens, 6-7 times per post, and nobody has checked whether caching works (COST-4).
- **Margin today.** A typical Basic Owner (15 posts a month, 9 of them 5-slide carousels) costs an estimated US$14-17 a month in variable cost. That is a 42-51% gross margin at US$29, before about US$80-90 a month of fixed platform cost (COST-10). The levers below, mostly small effort, bring it to about US$6 (about 80% margin).
- **Two gaps must close before real Owners use it.** Nothing records spend: `agent_runs` is never written and cached tokens are not logged (COST-1). Nothing enforces a monthly quota: the only limit is an in-memory cap of 5 designs a day, so a single Owner, Free or Basic, can cost US$100+ a month (COST-2).
- **Two price risks are coming.** gemini-3.8-flash doubles in price on 2027-01-01 (COST-11), and the 1,000 free WhatsApp service messages per number per month are shared by every Owner on the bot number (COST-7).
- **Confidence.** All figures come from call counts derived from the code and the team's single measured carousel run ([image-generation-providers.md](../image-generation-providers.md)), not from billing data. COST-1 should replace them with measured numbers within a week.

Assumptions behind the unit costs:

- **Orchestrator prefix:** about 10K tokens. - **Cache hit rate:** modelled at 0% (unverified today) and at 70%.
- **Not included:** payment fees, taxes (IVA) and support.

### Fixed floor, taxes and free-plan ceilings

- **Fixed floor once Neitor charges:** about US$80-90 a month: Vercel Pro 20, Supabase Pro 25, Kapso Pro 25 and Railway 10-20 (COST-10). At 20 Owners that is US$4.5 per Owner, so the Basic margin falls to 36% today and 64% optimized. At 100 Owners it is about US$0.9 per Owner. Break-even is about 4 Basic Owners.
- **IVA:** if US$29 is IVA-inclusive, the net is about US$23.8 and today's margin drops to about 29-41% (estimate from the synthesis, [summary.md](summary.md)). Payment fees (about 5%) are not included.
- **AI-only view:** the models dimension, with a 12-post profile, estimates about US$4.5-9.2 a month today, about US$7-16 from 2027-01-01 on all-Gemini, and about US$2.4-4.1 with its proposed model mix ([models.md](models.md)).
- **Free-plan ceilings the pilot will hit** (COST-10, COST-7):
  - Kapso Free (2,000 messages a month) runs out at about 10 active Owners, at about 200 messages each.
  - WhatsApp's 1,000 free service messages per number per month are shared by every Owner on the bot number, which is about 5 Basic Owners.
  - Gemini Tier 1's US$250 billing tier cap is about 35 Basic Owners at about US$6.8 of Gemini spend each. Tier 2 needs US$100 paid plus 3 days ([Gemini rate limits](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits), read 2026-10-19).
  - Supabase Free's 1 GB of storage fills at about 70-100 Businesses, because the Instagram import stores originals plus agent copies and chat photos are stored twice.
  - Vercel Hobby is for personal, non-commercial use, with hard caps and no paid overage, so it cannot serve a paid product's renders.

### Pricing implications

- **Biggest swings:** Zernio, image size and revisions move the margin the most, more than the choice of text model.
- **Image-model alternatives:** gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image costs the same US$0.0336 per 1K image and saves only about 8% per slide on input and thinking, with risk to multi-reference fidelity. It is not worth switching.

## Findings

### COST-1: No cost metering: `agent_runs` is never written, image usage is dropped and cache hits are not logged

- **Severity**: high · **Effort**: M · **Confidence**: high · **Verified**: adjusted
- **Evidence**: [0001_initial.sql:301](../../../backend/migrations/versions/0001_initial.sql) (the `agent_runs` table, with `cache_read_tokens`, exists; nothing in `backend/app` inserts into it, per grep); [llm/client.py:245-255](../../../backend/app/llm/client.py) (logs only input, output and thought tokens, with no `total_cached_tokens` and no `business_id`); [carousel/generator.py:108](../../../backend/app/carousel/generator.py) (each slide's usage is kept on `GeneratedSlide`) and [posts/maker.py:325-338](../../../backend/app/posts/maker.py) (then discarded; usage is only logged when a carousel fails, [carousel/generator.py:186-192](../../../backend/app/carousel/generator.py)); [mvp.md:105](../../product/mvp.md) (F7.2 agent run log is a Must) and [agents.md:37](../../architecture/agents.md) (each call should write `agent_runs` with cached tokens); [open-questions.md:58](../../open-questions.md) (the real cost per carousel is still unmeasured); [Gemini caching](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/caching) (cache hits are reported in `usage.total_cached_tokens`; read 2026-10-19).
- **Problem**: The team cannot see what an Owner costs, whether implicit caching works with `store=False`, or what a carousel really costs, just as the pilot forces the first money decisions (Basic and Pro limits, the Free cap). The estimate for a Basic Owner moves between US$14 and US$17 a month on the cache hit rate alone, and nobody can check it.
- **Recommendation**: Finish T10 as planned ([tasks.md:39](../../implementation/tasks.md)). Thread `business_id` and `job_id` through `ChatModel.reply`, `structured` and `grounded`, and through `ImageGenPort.generate`. Add a migration giving `agent_runs` `thought_tokens`, `image_size`, `image_count` and `cost_usd`. Write one row per text and image call, including the per-slide usage `PostMaker` drops today. Keep the price table, with `valid_from` dates, in config. Add a per-Business daily and monthly US$ view. Today: add `total_cached_tokens` to the `client.py` log line and log `GeneratedSlide.usage` on success.
- **Impact**: Turns every other finding into measured numbers. Makes the quotas in COST-2 enforceable. A broken cache, worth US$1-3 per Owner per month, would show up within a day.
- **Verifier note**: The code claims hold: grep finds `agent_runs` only in the migration and the docs. `GeneratedImage.usage` already includes `cached_tokens` ([imagegen/gemini.py:132-149](../../../backend/app/imagegen/gemini.py)) and rides on `GeneratedSlide`, but `PostMaker._render` throws it away; it is only logged when a carousel fails and in [carousel/cli.py](../../../backend/app/carousel/cli.py). The Gemini caching page names `usage.total_cached_tokens` and says the Interactions API supports implicit caching only. The auditor's "critical" overstated it: this is a known, tracked gap (F7.2 is a Must, and T10 says "business_id, job_id and agent_runs are still to do"), it breaks nothing for users, and Phase 1 is a single-Owner pilot. It stays high because pricing and quota decisions ([ADR 0031](../../decisions/0031-pricing-free-basic-pro.md), [open-questions.md:113](../../open-questions.md)) depend on it. The existing schema has `input_tokens`, `output_tokens`, `cache_read_tokens` and `effort` but no thought tokens, image size or count, or cost column, so the ledger needs the migration above.
- **Related**: [MOD-3](models.md), [ARCH-13](backend-architecture.md), [OPS-9](platform-ops.md), [DOC-7](docs-process.md), [AIQ-6](ai-quality.md)

### COST-6: "Cambiar" on a design regenerates every slide, even for a headline change; `redo()` is built but never called

- **Severity**: high · **Effort**: M · **Confidence**: high · **Verified**: confirmed
- **Evidence**: [conversation/tools.py:121-129](../../../backend/app/conversation/tools.py) (part `images` covers "headlines, colors, layout or scene"); [posts/maker.py:145-157](../../../backend/app/posts/maker.py) (`revise_post` with part `images` runs a full `_start`: all photos and a new drafter call); [posts/maker.py:319-338](../../../backend/app/posts/maker.py) (every remake runs a full `CarouselGenerator.generate`); [carousel/generator.py:65-90](../../../backend/app/carousel/generator.py) (`redo()` regenerates only chosen slides; nothing in `backend/app` calls it, per grep); [posts/store.py:26-31,36](../../../backend/app/posts/store.py) (a Post keeps only the rendered JPEGs, not the scenes, so a text change cannot be re-rendered); [ADR 0029](../../decisions/0029-designed-slides-ai-scene-code-layout.md) (slide redo "regenerates the scene and re-renders the slide") and [open-questions.md](../../open-questions.md) (Carousels: "Redo feedback that changes the words" is still open).
- **Problem**: Since ADR 0029, slide text is set by code, so changing a headline, kicker, body, colors or family needs no new image. Today each of those changes regenerates all N scenes: about US$0.47 for a 5-slide carousel, 40-70 s of waiting, and one slot of the daily cap. The Owner also has to re-check slides that changed when they did not ask for it. Picky Owners will hit this often.
