# Curated wood furniture and home stores in Uruguay: niche study

First niche for the weekly Niche Report ([ADR 0025](../decisions/0025-weekly-niche-report.md)). The focus is **curated, carefully made wood furniture**: stores and workshops like La Ibérica and KATEI that sell solid-wood, timeless or restored pieces with a design eye, not the price-led chains. The Niche Analyst works from these stores' **websites** and web search; Instagram is not used. All sources read 2026-10-18.

## Reference websites

The Niche Analyst reads these sites each week ([ADR 0025](../decisions/0025-weekly-niche-report.md)); Instagram accounts are not used.

### La Ibérica ([laiberica.com.uy](https://www.laiberica.com.uy/), mobile site [mobile.laiberica.com.uy](https://mobile.laiberica.com.uy/))

- Montevideo home store founded in **1892**, run by the family's third and fourth generations; once the name for wedding registries, it moved its focus to decor and to selling online nationwide ([InfoNegocios, 2019-08-13](https://infonegocios.biz/amp/bien-de-familia/la-iberica-127-anos-de-historia-y-7-familiares-trabajando-en-la-empresa)).
- Mission line: "Crear casas pensadas para el bienestar", with advice "adaptada a tus necesidades funcionales y estilo de vida" and an emotional register ("pequeñas alegrías todos los días") ([mobile site](https://mobile.laiberica.com.uy/)).
- Products name the material: "OLMO MACIZO", "ROBLE MACIZO", "HAYA MACIZA", "PINO MACIZO RECICLADO"; fresno armchairs with linen; Italian leather; linen and tussor sofas; papel maché lamps. Prices shown in pesos and dollars, from accessories to sofas around $78,000–88,800 ([laiberica.com.uy](https://www.laiberica.com.uy/), [mobile site](https://mobile.laiberica.com.uy/)).
- Content blocks: "Novedades & Destacados", "Ambientes que inspiran", design tips ("Seguí la regla del 80/20": 80% timeless base, 20% pieces with personality; colour, shape, texture and material as connecting threads), "Back in stock" (roble, Italian leather), new collections (papel maché, rattan), gift guide and wedding registries ([mobile site](https://mobile.laiberica.com.uy/)).
- Selling: WhatsApp as the assisted-sales channel, free shipping above a threshold, a gift card for every $50,000 spent; **no cuotas or financing up front** ([mobile site](https://mobile.laiberica.com.uy/)).
- Tone: warm, aspirational, close, Rioplatense *vos* ("Seguí", "Recibí").

### KATEI ([kateiuy.lovable.app](https://kateiuy.lovable.app))

- "Muebles con Alma": a Uruguayan brand of **antique and period furniture restored by hand**; "Piezas rescatadas que conservan la nobleza de la madera y el rastro de la mano humana" ([kateiuy.lovable.app](https://kateiuy.lovable.app)).
- Each piece is a post in a dated feed with photo, measurements, wood (cedro, roble, pinotea, curupay, raíz), year, origin (France, Spain, England, Austria, Italy, Uruguay), condition score ("9 / 10"), story and price (e.g. an oak sideboard at $U 25.900); sold pieces stay visible as "Vendido" (same source).
- Voice: first person, informal and funny, Rioplatense *vos*, mixing the restorer's personal notes with short history lessons on styles (Luis XV, Biedermeier, Art Nouveau) (same source).
- Photos: each piece styled in a real room; one post uses an AI-generated image (same source).

## What sets this segment apart

- **Material and origin are the message**: the wood is named (roble, olmo, haya, cedro, eucalipto) and its origin, age or drying is explained (La Ibérica, KATEI, Marvic above). The mass chains lead with cuotas, free shipping and "llega mañana" instead (Divino and Balton sites: [divino.com.uy](https://www.divino.com.uy), [balton.com.uy](https://balton.com.uy/)), which this segment doesn't.
- **Advice and atmosphere**: rooms that inspire, design rules, integral advice (La Ibérica); made to measure for the customer's real space (Marvic).
- **Trust through craft**: warranty, the workshop, delivery and assembly by the maker (Marvic); the restorer's own hand and story behind each piece (KATEI).
- **Formats**: the sites suggest styled rooms, material close-ups, design tips and one-piece stories; for Instagram formats in general, see [instagram-content-trends.md](instagram-content-trends.md) (carousels for saves, Reels for reach) and the carousel shapes in [carousel-references.md](carousel-references.md) ("Cómo cuidar la madera maciza" fits this segment directly).

## Not the focus: price-led chains

Sodimac, Divino, Balton, La Cueva, ComboHome sell on price, cuotas and fast delivery ([divino.com.uy](https://www.divino.com.uy), [balton.com.uy](https://balton.com.uy/), [ComboHome blog](https://combohome.uy/blogs/news/las-9-mejores-mueblerias-de-uruguay)). They are useful only as contrast; the Niche Report should not push their tone onto a curated Owner.

## Implications for build101

- **The Brand Brain's materials are the story**: the copy should name the wood and how the piece is made, but only from the Brand Brain, the Catalog or the Owner's words ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#building-the-mvp)); the agents never invent a species, origin or drying time.
- **Tone**: calm, advice-led, timeless ("cómo cuidar", "cómo combinar", "por qué roble"); avoid urgency and price-led promos unless the Owner asks.
- **Images**: warm natural light, wood grain and texture close-ups, styled rooms; matches the regional 2026 trends in Market context and the Art Director's scene choices.
- **Formats to try first**: care and material carousels, design-tip carousels (the 80/20 rule shape), one-piece stories (origin, wood, restoration), workshop process, styled rooms.
