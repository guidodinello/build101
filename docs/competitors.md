# Competitive landscape

Grouped by what they replace for the owner. Evidence and sources for each row: [research/competitive-landscape.md](research/competitive-landscape.md) (read 2026-10-17).

## AI marketing / content tools (owner-facing, dashboard-first)

| Player | Notes |
|---|---|
| Sintra (incl. its social media "helper"), Marblism, Lindy | "AI employees" with a marketing role (Marblism's "Sonny" creates on-brand social posts); web dashboards, English-first, US solopreneurs, a cast of named characters the owner manages |
| Predis.ai, Ocoya, Simplified, Jasper | AI post/caption generators with scheduling; the owner still operates a web tool |
| Canva (Magic Studio) | Design + AI writing + scheduling; powerful but requires learning the tool |
| Meta Business Suite / Meta AI features | Native scheduling and AI caption suggestions; free, but the owner does the planning and the work |
| Shopify Sidekick | Owner-facing assistant with approve-before-apply; only for Shopify stores |

## Messaging-native agents

| Player | Facing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Meta Business Agent | Customer | Native to WhatsApp Business, generally available since June 2026; answers the business's customers. Complementary to us |
| Leadsales, Yavendio, Agenteala, Wa2Desk | Customer | LatAm WhatsApp sales/support agents |
| Poke (poke.com, not pokee.ai), OpenClaw | Individual | Personal agents over messaging, not SMB- or marketing-specific. Poke is the reference for proactive "texts you first" |

## Human alternatives

- Freelance community managers and small agencies: the real competitor for budget. They're trusted and personal, but expensive and slow for a micro business.

## The gap

An **agency-like team of agents** that runs the owner's marketing **inside WhatsApp**, in **Rioplatense Spanish**, for **non-technical owners**, with **approval before anything goes out**. The tools above make owners operate software, and the WhatsApp agents talk to customers, not to the owner about their marketing.

Pitch line: *Sintra gives you a team you have to manage. We give you one person who manages the team for you.* One face (the account manager), with the specialist team visible but never something the owner has to coordinate.
